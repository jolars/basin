# Bounded full-TRF analytic measurement pilot

This [manifest](2026-10-09-bounded-trf-001.toml) and [retained
report](../bounded-trf-results.json) extend step 5 with 32 full-TRF solves: four
fixtures, two precisions, and physical caps 0, 1, 2, and 4000. Source and
planned manifest commit `e2c9a6a` preceded execution. No defaults, safeguards,
quality targets for calibration, or holdout outcomes changed.

## Fixtures and independent checks

Three fixtures use identity residuals `r=(x0-1,x1+1)`, with half-RSS as the
objective. Their unique constrained solutions are the coordinatewise projections
of `(1,-1)` onto their boxes:

- Active bounds: `[0,0.5] × [-0.5,0]`, start `(0.1,-0.1)`, solution
  `(0.5,-0.5)`.
- Mixed fixed/free: `{0.25} × [-2,0]`, start `(0.25,-0.1)`, solution
  `(0.25,-1)`.
- All fixed: `{0.25} × {-0.25}`, initialized at the only feasible point.

The fourth fixture uses `r=(2-x0²,x1+1)` on `[-1,1] × [-2,0]`, starting at
`(0,-1)`. The constrained minima are `(±1,-1)`, with objective 0.5. The start
has objective 2 and zero gradient, but is a nonminimum stationary point. It
checks that native convergence does not automatically confer minimum quality.

The independent checker evaluates objectives with Decimal arithmetic, checks
publication feasibility, reconstructs the free-coordinate gradient and the
Coleman–Li stopping metric, verifies trial predictions with the analytic
Jacobian, and reconciles physical and logical calls. Identity-fixture quality
checks use parameter and unit-step projected KKT errors at unit scale, with
limits `1e-6` for `f64` and `1e-3` for `f32`. These are measurement checks, not
selected convergence thresholds or CDP-1 corpus eligibility certificates.

## Outcomes

The unrestricted active-bound and mixed-fixed `f64` solves converge in 13 and 15
physical calls, with parameter errors below `4e-12`. Their `f32` counterparts
return accurate points but retain different native outcomes:

- Active bounds: `Failed`, finite feasible model construction/solve failure,
  after 13 calls; parameter and projected KKT errors `5.96e-8`.
- Mixed fixed/free: `Stalled`, contracted trial changes no parameter after a
  finite equal-cost rejection, after 17 calls; both errors `1.85e-6`.

All-fixed cases with positive caps converge structurally after one residual
call, with no Jacobian request. Stationary controls with positive caps converge
by scaled gradient after one fused residual/Jacobian call, while independently
failing minimum quality: parameter distance 1 and objective gap 1.5.

All eight zero-cap cases fail initialization. Eight insufficient-cap active or
mixed-fixed cases fail callbacks and have no returned point. Four native
accepted trials fail their subsequent Jacobian request before publication.
Across all cases, the verifier retains 14 convergence outcomes, one failure, one
stall, eight initialization errors, and eight callback errors. It checks 37
attempted trials, including four denied callbacks, 29 accepted trials, four
finite rejections, and 25 published trials.

## Verification and limits

All 17 measurement tests pass on nalgebra 0.34 and 0.35. Diagnostics preserve
exact trajectories, costs, counts, and outcomes in both precisions. Six Python
tests check reference arithmetic, quality rejection, and seven corrupted-record
controls. Workspace all-target/all-feature clippy with warnings denied and Rust
formatting pass.

The first quality pass incorrectly required good-quality returns to report
convergence. The verifier now preserves good failed/stalled outcomes; solve
source and CSVs remain unchanged. An existing mutation control was adjusted to
alter a converged row rather than assuming the first row had converged.

This covers small analytic full-TRF boxes only. Legacy bounded TRF, robust
losses, finite differences, transformed cases, rank deficiency, other backends,
and complete inner-work accounting remain open. The stationary control
illustrates the limits of first-order stopping; it supplies no tolerance fix.
Next, add robust-loss LM/TRF fixtures with independently known robust minima and
gradients, retaining these bounded cases as measurement controls.
